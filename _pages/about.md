I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Computer, Electrical, and Mathematical Sciences and Engineering Division at King Abdullah University of Science and Technology ([KAUST](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/en/](https://cemse.kaust.edu.sa/ )), where I work with Professor [Paula Moraga](https://www.paulamoraga.com/) on developing innovative statistical methods and computational tools for geospatial data analysis and health surveillance. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the [University of Calgary](https://ucalgary.ca/), collaborating with Professors [Rob Deardon](https://robdeardon.github.io/) and [Alexandra Schmidt](https://alex-schmidt.research.mcgill.ca/) on modeling complex behavioral change in epidemic systems. I earned my PhD in Statistics in 2024 from the [Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile](https://www.mat.uc.cl/), under the supervision of Professors [Fernando Quintana](http://www.mat.uc.cl/~quintana/) and [Rosangela Loschi](https://www.est.ufmg.br/~loschi/).

My research interests center on Bayesian inference, both parametric and nonparametric, with a particular focus on modeling and computational techniques for complex and correlated data. Much of my work is applied to infectious disease modeling and spatio-temporal disease mapping.

**Research interests**: 
- Bayesian inference; 
- Biostatistics; 
- Clustering; 
- Computational statistics; 
- Random partition models; 
- Spatio-temporal models.
